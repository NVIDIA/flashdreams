harbor_market first-frame provenance

Artwork generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using
harbor_market_spawn_geometry.png as the calibrated camera reference.
Original artwork is retained at ../../tools/harbor_market_artwork.png.

The final 1280x704 seed is deterministically composited. The camera and road
geometry come from this map's default compiled spawn. Generated asphalt is
used only as texture. Road surfaces, curbs, white and yellow markings use
canonical f-theta ground projection. Paint polylines are read from the compiled archive with the runtime Ludus
lane-line loader, including its 3-metre dash / 9-metre gap pattern, sampled
endpoints and simplified solid groups.
Foreground scenery occludes disconnected distant ground surfaces. The silver
hood is retained as a foreground layer occupying roughly the lower fifth.

Rebuild in the app environment from the repository root:
python apps/crazy_robotaxi/tools/compose_map_frames.py harbor_market

The script checks paint against runtime-loaded lane geometry, writes the initial
frame, and saves a cyan semantic alignment overlay plus raw geometry preview.
This is a static geometry check, not an Omnidreams motion validation.
The previous compositor used 6-metre gaps; that did not match runtime.
This offline artwork prompt is specific to the spawn; it is not used as the
runtime static prompt, which must remain valid while the car moves and turns.

Artwork-generation prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: Harbor Market taxi-map initial-frame artwork.
Convert the provided calibrated road render into a beautiful realistic working waterfront neighborhood in soft clear morning daylight. Preserve the wide-lens forward road view, flat four-lane avenue, horizon at 49.3% image height, and asphalt silhouette. Output landscape 1280:704 aspect ratio. Camera is just right of road center. Road goes straight to a distant intersection; no nearby bends.
Distinct setting: attractive red-brick Victorian market halls and restored dock warehouses on both sides, deep green canvas shop awnings, small cafes, leafy street trees, black iron lamps and broad pale sidewalks. Slender harbor cranes and a few ship masts can rise above distant roofs. A coherent human-scale port neighborhood, warm brick, restrained green accents, lively but uncluttered. Buildings and market stalls stay beyond sidewalks, never on the road. No ocean filling the roadway. Morning sun, blue sky with a few light clouds, realistic detailed asphalt grain and softly shaded facades.
The exact road markings will be composited afterward: leave the asphalt completely unmarked. NO white/yellow paint, crosswalks, stop bars, text, arrows, parking bays or side driveways. NO cars or pedestrians on the road. Avoid strong shadows crossing the whole road.
Add a curved pale silver taxi hood plainly visible across the bottom 18% of the frame, with a black wiper recess along the bottom. Hood leading edge at 82% image height at center, curving gently down toward lower corners. No dashboard, driver, steering wheel, chase view, HUD, logos, readable text or watermark. Crisp natural photographic frame.
