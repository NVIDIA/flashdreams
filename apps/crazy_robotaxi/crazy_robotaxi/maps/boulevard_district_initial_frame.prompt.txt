boulevard_district first-frame provenance

Artwork generated with the built-in image_gen tool, using
boulevard_district_spawn_geometry.png as the calibrated camera reference.
Original artwork is retained at ../../tools/boulevard_district_artwork.png.

The final 1280x704 seed is deterministically composited. The camera and road
geometry come from this map's default compiled spawn. Generated asphalt is
used only as texture. Road surfaces, curbs, white and yellow markings use
canonical f-theta ground projection. Paint polylines are read from the compiled archive with the runtime Ludus
lane-line loader, including its 3-metre dash / 9-metre gap pattern, sampled
endpoints and simplified solid groups.
Foreground scenery occludes disconnected distant ground surfaces. The silver
hood is retained as a foreground layer occupying roughly the lower fifth.

Rebuild in the app environment from the repository root:
python apps/crazy_robotaxi/tools/compose_map_frames.py boulevard_district

The script checks paint against runtime-loaded lane geometry, writes the initial
frame, and saves a cyan semantic alignment overlay plus raw geometry preview.
This is a static geometry check, not an Omnidreams motion validation.
The previous compositor used 6-metre gaps; that did not match runtime.
